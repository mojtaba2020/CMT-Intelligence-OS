# CMT Intelligence OS — Controlled Pilot Proposal

**Purpose:** Test whether a separate discrepancy-screening workflow can improve quality review before approved reports are prepared.

**What is available today:** A synthetic-only demonstration for NDG and fresh-concrete evidence comparison, independently documented exceptions, and an offline review package. This prototype is NOT connected to ElmTree, does NOT read photos automatically, and is NOT approved for live customer work.

## Suggested minimum experiment (only after written approval)

- Select 20–30 authorized, anonymized records representing both ordinary and difficult cases.
- Have a qualified individual independently establish a reference transcription for each source.
- Use the same record types to measure manual time and assisted time, including correction and engineer-review effort.
- Track missed critical discrepancies, false alerts, source-identity errors, unit errors, operator correction time, and total time.
- Report separate results per test type and include unsuccessful trials; do not estimate savings from synthetic samples.
- Maintain existing engineering review and ElmTree reporting as the system of record; no automated write-back.

## Initial go/no-go criteria (pilot targets, NOT engineering standards)

- No uncorrected critical source-identity or safety-related transcription errors in accepted pilot records.
- Every result linked to the authorized evidence and independent reviewer.
- Net assisted workflow time measured, including corrections, and compared to manual baseline without cherry-picking.
- Written technical, legal and IT authorization before any later live integration.

**Decision requested:** Permission to discuss a small, isolated sandbox/anonymous-data pilot and identify an internal technical sponsor. No access to production ElmTree is needed for this discussion.
