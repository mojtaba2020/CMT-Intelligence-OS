Compatible input columns

Names are matched case-insensitively; spaces/underscores are normalized. Missing readings are rejected, not guessed. Units must match the column descriptions.

Common: type, project, tech, location, date, evidence, specRef

Soil: wet (pcf), moisture (%), mdd (pcf), minComp (%), proctor

Concrete: slump (in), slumpMin, slumpMax (in), air (%), airMin, airMax (%), temp, tempMax (°F), mix

Expanded v0.6 catalog: use exact field keys from the Test catalog tab and the downloadable CSV template. Advanced multi-row worksheets (Proctor curve, sieve stack, cylinder set) must be entered in tab 08; use JSON backup for complete round-trip. CSV export includes serialized row arrays.

Privacy & engineering: Use only synthetic or permitted, non-confidential data. An imported CSV does not prove method compliance, measurement accuracy, source calibration or authorization. All records require human checks. Browser-local audit history is editable.

Three distinct questions

Does form match gauge?
Wet/dry density and moisture are normalized to pcf and percent; only transcription tolerances are used

Does form match project references?

3
What needs an engineer?
Any discrepancy or missing identity goes into the reviewer queue; no automated approval or ASTM certification
Important: The 0.10 pcf / 0.10 percentage-point starting tolerances are editable illustrative transcription thresholds, not ASTM tolerances or project acceptance limits. If gauge and paper round differently, the reviewer must assess precision manually.
